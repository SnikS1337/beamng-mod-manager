Crash Test Dummy 1.1

75KG Ragdoll Humanoid

Tags:
  • prop
  • Overview
  • Reviews (63)
  • Version History
  • Discussion
  1. Crash test dummy available for download! This ragdoll humanoid weighs 75kg and will stand up until you hit it. The Jbeam is fairly light and allows multiple dummies to be spawned without FPS drop. 3D model by myself, although feel free to use it however you want.

    How to spawn the dummy correctly:
    1. Spawn the prop
    2. Press J to pause the physics
    3. Press R to reset position
    4. Press J again to unpause physics
